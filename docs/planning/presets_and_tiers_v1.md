Presets and Tiers (v1) — Audiophile Language, Behavior Intent

1. Subwoofer Presets (Sealed)
These are user-friendly tonal/behavior intents, not exact EQ curves.
Tight & Accurate
- Emphasis: control, less boom
- Expected effect: slightly reduced low-end weight, improved perceived “speed”
Balanced (default)
- Emphasis: neutral
Warm & Full
- Emphasis: more mid-bass warmth (musical fullness)
Room-Filling
- Emphasis: more overall bass presence (with appropriate warnings for neighbors/open-plan)

2. Subwoofer Presets (Ported)
Tight & Controlled
- More restraint around tuning, avoids boom
Balanced (default)
Deep & Smooth
- Emphasis: stronger deep-bass around/near Fb (with leakiness considerations)
Big & Bold
- Emphasis: maximum presence (with stronger warning prompts)

3. “Lowest Strong Bass (Hz)” Guidance (Locked)
Use this for sealed subs and mains:
- Defined as “lowest frequency that feels solid/authoritative, not just audible.”
- Provide quick-picks: 20/25/30/35/40
- “Not sure” sets reasonable default and lowers confidence slightly.

4. Treatment Strength Tiers (Light/Medium/Heavy) — Per Type
Corner Bass Trap (porous)
- Light: modest improvement, mostly upper bass; mild ringing reduction
- Medium: noticeable 40–90 improvements; reduced peakiness and ringing
- Heavy: best porous-trap low-frequency reach; strongest decay reduction

Rear Wall Absorber (thick porous)
- Light: helps 60–120 more
- Medium: strong 40–100 decay improvement for length modes
- Heavy: broad improvement, especially in closed rooms

Thick Panel (broadband)
- Light: mostly upper bass improvements
- Medium: modest smoothing/decay improvements
- Heavy: best upper-bass control; still not “deep bass magic”

Tuned Trap
- Light: mild, narrower impact
- Medium: clear, moderate-band impact (default)
- Heavy: strongest, slightly broader / more robust effect

5. Treatment Snapping Rules (Locked)
- Corner traps: corners only
- Rear wall absorber: rear wall only
- Thick panels: walls only (front/rear/side segments)
- Tuned traps: corners + walls; suggested zones depend on target frequency/region

6. Diminishing Returns UI Expression (Locked)
Impact Preview card
- Zone saturation meter + Frequency overlap meter
- Zone icons: Best / OK / Limited (frequency-specific)
- “Doubling up” tooltip on same zone + overlapping targets

7. Tuned Trap Overlap (Locked)
- ±5 Hz → heavy overlap
- ±10 Hz → some overlap
- ±10 Hz → none
